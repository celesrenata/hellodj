Everything You Need to DJ Your Server

From casual listening to full-blown visual experiences — HelloDJ scales to your needs.

Multi-Source Playback

YouTube, Spotify, Tidal HiFi, SoundCloud, Deezer, and Apple Music. Search across all sources or pick your favorite.

Video Streaming

Music videos and visualizers streamed directly into Discord via Activity. HLS transcoded in real-time with hardware acceleration.

Audio Visualizers

GPU-accelerated Milkdrop-style visualizers with multipass feedback rendering. Preset crossfades sync to the beat.

Voice Activated

Custom "Hello DJ" wake word with speech-to-text and AI intent recognition. Control playback without typing.

Web Player

Control your bot from any browser. Search, queue, drag-to-reorder, volume control — all with real-time WebSocket sync.

Your Own Instance

Dedicated bot pod with isolated queue, sessions, and config. No shared resources, no interference from other servers.
